# State-aware optical-transfer method discovery

The original all-environment optical-feature injection failed its frozen
development gate. The 96 blind hydrogen-evolution outcomes remain unopened.
This new stage is explicitly post-gate method discovery and cannot rewrite the
failed result.

The job first trains donor models separately in water/methanol,
water/small-alcohol and self-hosted solid-state records. Each state-property
pair must independently pass the original source-only scaffold-validation
gate. The recipient benchmark then evaluates global, state-specific and
aqueous-plus-solid donor blocks using direct augmentation, property-family
restriction, support gating, residual correction and late fusion. Shuffled
donors and equal-dimensional Gaussian features are retained as controls.

Three hundred outcome-independent scaffold draws are used at each recipient
label budget of 30, 60 and 120. For every random-forest candidate and draw,
predictions are averaged over 20 forest seeds. These are computational
sensitivity analyses, not additional experimental samples.

This stage makes no significance claim. A candidate must improve mean RMSE by
at least 2% at the primary budget of 60, improve at least 60% of draws, remain
non-harmful on average at budgets 30 and 120, and beat the corresponding
shuffled donor. Candidates are ranked by their minimum gain across the three
budgets. If none passes, the programme abstains. If one passes, exactly one
fully specified strategy may be frozen before the blind outcomes are opened.
