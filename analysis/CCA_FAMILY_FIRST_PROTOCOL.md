# Outcome-unseen CCA family-first validation protocol

This protocol is the next claim-bearing experiment selected by the completed
Caltech method-development analyses. It is not a report of another positive
result.

The complete policy must be frozen before target outcomes are downloaded or
revealed. Source credibility is decided from leakage-safe grouped evidence and
wrong/shuffled controls. Every admitted source remains an independent rank;
rank consensus supplies joint support, while round-robin exposes unique source
proposals. The first acquisition pass takes one representative per predeclared
formula/provenance or composition-cluster component. OOD distance may define a
scope, quota, or tie-breaker, but it cannot multiply the source score. Failed
sources abstain and target-only novelty/uniform channels remain available.

The co-primary outcomes are distinct-component AUC through acquisition 20 and
acquisitions to the first distinct top component. Entity recall is mandatory,
not secondary decoration, because it exposes the cost of prioritizing breadth.
The full portfolio must beat uniform, composition novelty, the best admitted
single source, wrong-source and shuffled-source portfolios, with Holm control
inside the target. Seed intervals alone are insufficient: uncertainty must
come from another dataset, article block, time block, or prospective campaign
block.

Before outcomes, write at least one candidate-region card containing the source
rank evidence, proposed composition/structure/condition relation, matched
target-only control, falsifier, and planned mechanistic measurements. A positive
ranking without this card supports screening but not scientific inspiration.
The machine-readable freeze template is
`cca_family_first_outcome_unseen_protocol.json`.
