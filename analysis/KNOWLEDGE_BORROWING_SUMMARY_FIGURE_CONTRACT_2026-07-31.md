# Knowledge-borrowing summary figure contract

**Core conclusion:** Neighbouring experimental programmes rescue selected
data-poor OOD decisions only when the transferred relation or ranking is
matched to experimental state and passes explicit falsification gates.

**Figure archetype:** Schematic-led composite.

**Target output:** Broad-audience Chinese summary graphic; 183 mm by 103 mm;
editable SVG and PDF plus 300 dpi PNG and 600 dpi TIFF.

**Backend:** Python with Matplotlib only.

**Panel map:**

- Left: three forms of neighbouring experimental knowledge.
- Centre: sparse OOD recipient and the four gate families.
- Right hero: accepted numerical prediction and accepted ordinal screening,
  followed by the frozen programme-specific rejection.
- Bottom strip: the 0/40 generic-injection result explains why routing is
  necessary.

**Evidence hierarchy:**

1. Hero evidence: five-anchor ranking improves from rho 0.537 to 0.910.
2. Independent task evidence: unseen-salt log-RMSE falls by 28.64% with raw
   R2 0.629.
3. Specificity boundary: the frozen second programme rejects the donor.
4. Motivation: no generic donor-feature edge passes the complete gate.

**Statistics shown:** Point estimates and the primary ranking interval only;
the figure does not treat repeated seeds as independent experiments.

**Source data:** Verified compact result summaries represented in
`figures/source_data/knowledge_borrowing_summary_zh.csv`.

**Reviewer risk:** The graphic must not imply universal transfer or prospective
laboratory discovery. The rejection route and retrospective-decision wording
remain visible.
