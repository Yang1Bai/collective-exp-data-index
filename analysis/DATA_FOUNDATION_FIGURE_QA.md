# Data-foundation figure QA record

## Claim and archetype

**Core conclusion.** Every resource displayed in the manuscript enters a
numerical analysis. Twenty-one experimental resources are analysed; 20 participate
in directed knowledge borrowing and one supplies an independent artifact gate.
The empirical portfolio retains favorable and unfavorable outcomes.

The figure is an asymmetric mixed-modality overview. Panel a is the hero
resource-role matrix, panel b reports the normalized measurement core, panel c
fixes the benchmark denominator, and panel d displays the all-program evidence
portfolio. Python/matplotlib produced the preview and every export.

## Source and denominator QA

- The inventory contains exactly 21 unique resources. The analytical layers are
  13 normalized resources, seven frozen external resources, and one streamed
  analysis-only resource.
- The directed role columns contain 16 donors and 17 recipients. Thirteen
  resources appear in both roles, so their union is 20 rather than 33. NIST
  ISODB is the remaining artifact-gate-only resource.
- The normalized-layer table contains all 13 integrated resources. Their
  measurement counts sum to 96,184. No resource or measurement row was sampled
  or removed for display.
- The figure reports 230 globally distinct property labels and 29,516 globally
  distinct canonical entities from the release manifest. The sum of per-source
  property counts is 231 because one property label occurs in more than one
  resource; that sum is not displayed as a global distinct count.
- The directed-evidence denominator is read from the completed leave-one-
  programme benchmark: 97 evaluated edges, 20 target tasks, and 13 programme
  clusters.
- The 97 edges are heterogeneous tests rather than 97 independent
  cross-database replications. They include within-resource cross-property,
  adjacent-condition, external cross-dataset, OOD-ranking, and control edges.
- The portfolio matrix is a categorical rendering of the frozen decisions
  reported in the manuscript claim table and compact summaries. Empty cells
  mean that an endpoint was not the claim-bearing test for that programme.
- Exactly five programmes are marked role-defining: ESTM to OBELiX, KIT
  temperature transfer, OBELiX/ESTM to Caltech, and battery Stage 1 to Stage 2.
  Their roles are respectively an internal directional cross-domain candidate,
  a gate-passed local rescue, an external ranking-to-policy boundary, and a
  temporal method-development diagnostic. SpecGen OER B/D adds the controlled
  complete-system relation-transfer demonstration. They are not presented as
  five independent successes.

## Interpretation guard

The analysed cohort is broad enough to test different borrowing mechanisms, but
the resources are not exchangeable evidence for a universal transfer law. The
empirical claim is attached to directed tasks, independent and outcome-unseen
programmes, a temporal test, and retained null or harmful boundaries.
