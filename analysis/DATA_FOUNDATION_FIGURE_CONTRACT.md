# Data-foundation figure contract

- **Core conclusion:** every resource shown in the paper enters at least one
  numerical analysis. Twenty-one experimental resources are analysed; 20 form the
  directed knowledge-borrowing network and one supplies an independent
  compensation-artifact gate. Positive, null, harmful, unresolved, and
  non-evaluable outcomes remain in the same empirical denominator.
- **Archetype:** asymmetric mixed-modality quantitative overview with a
  resource-role matrix as the hero panel.
- **Backend:** Python/matplotlib exclusively for drawing, previewing, export,
  and visual QA.
- **Target/output:** *Digital Discovery*, 171 mm double-column width; editable
  SVG and PDF plus 600 dpi TIFF and 300 dpi PNG.
- **Panel a -- analysed resource cohort:** all 21 experimental resources are
  named and assigned their actual analytical roles. The role matrix contains
  16 donors, 17 recipients, 13 resources used in both directions, and one
  artifact-gate-only resource. Layer codes distinguish 13 normalized resources,
  seven frozen external resources, and one streamed analysis-only resource.
- **Panel b -- normalized measurement core:** all 13 normalized resources and
  all 96,184 measurements are retained. Bars are log-scaled only to keep the
  599 to 26,025 resource range visible; exact counts are printed.
- **Panel c -- empirical denominator:** the 21-resource cohort, 20
  transfer-active resources, 97 evaluated directed edges, 20 target tasks, and
  13 programme clusters in the systematic gate benchmark are stated without
  introducing an unused catalog denominator.
- **Panel d -- evidence portfolio:** every major claim-bearing family is shown
  across coefficient transport, few-shot prediction, OOD screening, and
  sequential or temporal endpoints. Bold labels mark five role-defining
  examples; they are not the only analysed programmes and are not selected by
  favorable sign.
- **Evidence hierarchy:** a establishes exactly what data entered the study and
  in which direction; b shows the measurement scale of the normalized core; c
  fixes the inferential denominator; d shows that the conclusion rests on
  selective effects and retained boundaries rather than cherry-picked
  successes.
- **Statistics:** this overview reports frozen counts and categorical decisions;
  effect sizes, independent units, confidence intervals, multiplicity control,
  and decision gates remain in the claim-specific figures and source tables.
- **Source data:** `figure_data_foundation_inventory.csv`,
  `figure_data_foundation_lake.csv`, `figure_data_foundation_scope.csv`, and
  `figure_data_foundation_portfolio.csv`.
- **Image integrity:** every count and decision symbol is generated from the
  frozen source tables; no manual graphical alteration.
- **Reviewer risk:** the 21 resources are not interchangeable replicates and the
  97 edges include within-resource cross-property, adjacent-condition,
  cross-database, OOD-ranking, and control tests. The figure therefore supports
  a heterogeneous, edge-selective method evaluation rather than a claim of
  universal transfer across 21 independent fields.
