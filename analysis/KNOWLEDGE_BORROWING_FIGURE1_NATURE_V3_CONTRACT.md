# Figure 1 Nature v3 figure contract

## Scientific message

Neighbouring experimental databases are not transferred wholesale. A donor can
support a sparse out-of-distribution recipient only when a specific relation or
candidate order survives four checks: common inputs, matched experimental state,
a declared physical relation, and matched falsifiers. The accepted object is then
routed to absolute prediction or ordinal screening; otherwise the method abstains.

## Target venue and archetype

- Target: Nature-family double-column main figure.
- Archetype: schematic-led asymmetric mixed-modality figure.
- Final size: 183 mm wide, approximately 114 mm high.
- Dominant panel: one continuous left-to-right scientific workflow occupying
  approximately two thirds of the figure height.
- Supporting panel: one compact evidence strip with three mutually exclusive
  decisions: predict, screen, or abstain.

## Panel plan

### a. Qualifying the transferable object

- Three neighbouring experimental programmes are represented by compact,
  domain-specific measurement curves rather than decorative database icons.
- Candidate links enter a four-stage contract: common inputs, experimental
  state, physical relation, and falsifiers.
- Rejected paths terminate at the first failed check.
- Only a narrow relation/order signal crosses into a sparse OOD recipient.
- Five measured anchors and an unmeasured candidate frontier make the OOD task
  explicit.

### b. Evidence-supported routing

- Predict: an unseen-salt electrolyte relation lowers log-RMSE by 28.64% and
  yields raw R2 = 0.629 and Spearman rho = 0.871.
- Screen: five target labels improve candidate ordering from rho = 0.537 to
  0.910 and high-performance-quartile precision from 0.490 to 0.933.
- Abstain: no generic donor edge passes the complete gate (0/40), and a frozen
  donor score of 0.694 loses to the recipient-only score of 0.783.

## Visual language

- White background; no gradients, shadows, or decorative textures.
- Arial/Helvetica, editable text, 5-7 pt body text, 8 pt bold lowercase panel
  labels.
- Dark neutral text. Colour encodes marks and decisions only: teal/green for
  accepted evidence, orange for unmeasured candidates, coral for rejection,
  navy/blue for measured recipient evidence.
- Thin grey construction lines and generous whitespace.
- One visual hero: the qualified relation crossing the contract into the OOD
  frontier.

## Provenance and export

- All numerical annotations are read from a committed source-data CSV.
- Exports: editable SVG and PDF, 300 dpi PNG, 600 dpi LZW TIFF.
- Required QA: strict script validation, PDF rendering through Poppler,
  full-size visual inspection, and half-size readability inspection.
