## Scientific mapping

- Panel **a** is explicitly schematic. The three object classes illustrate the
  breadth of possible experimental programmes; they are not counts of analysed
  databases. The four gates correspond to representation, experimental state,
  transferable relation and falsification. Blue filled marks are measured
  recipient samples; orange open marks are OOD candidates.
- Panel **b** reports the frozen systematic benchmark: 8 recipients × 5 real
  donor edges, with 0 of 40 passing the complete OOD-repair gate.
- Panel **c** reproduces the reported relative RMSE reductions and 95%
  intervals: 16.3% [9.2, 22.9], 26.1% [20.0, 31.7], 28.64% [24.03, 33.52],
  3.2% [-1.8, 9.7], and -10.4% [-17.2, -3.4]. Positive values mean error
  reduction.
- Panel **d** reproduces the accepted five-label ranking contrast (0.537 to
  0.910; delta rho 0.374) and the frozen rejection (donor 0.694 versus
  recipient-only 0.783).

## Visual QA

- The final PDF was rendered with Poppler at 220 dpi and inspected at full
  width. Panel letters, headings, gate labels, interval marks and axis labels
  are legible; no objects or text overlap after the final panel-spacing pass.
- The design is schematic-led and asymmetric, with the hero panel occupying
  more area than any quantitative panel. Related elements are grouped by
  whitespace rather than enclosing cards.
- Colour is redundant with position, fill state and route labels; green/red is
  not the sole carrier of any numerical conclusion.
