# Figure 1 image-generation prompt

## Base prompt

Create a clean English scientific workflow figure for a high-impact materials
informatics methods paper. Use a wide white canvas with four modules:

1. `DATA-POOR RECIPIENT`: measured points and distinct OOD candidates.
2. `QUALIFIED NEIGHBOURING EDGE`: donor and recipient experimental programmes
   connected by `SHARED REPRESENTATION`, `FALSIFIABLE RELATION`, and
   `EXPERIMENTAL STATE`.
3. `ENDPOINT ROUTING`: a gate containing `VALIDITY`, `UTILITY`, `ROBUSTNESS`,
   and `SPECIFICITY`, branching to `NUMERICAL PREDICTION`, `CANDIDATE RANKING`,
   and `ABSTAIN`.
4. `EVIDENCE AND BOUNDARY`: cards containing `LOG-RMSE DOWN 28.64%`,
   `rho: 0.537 TO 0.910`, and `FROZEN RECIPIENT: REJECT, 0.694 < 0.783`.

Add a lower strip: `GENERIC DONOR FEATURE: 0 / 40 EDGES PASSED`.

Use a restrained low-saturation navy, teal, amber, green, and muted-red
palette, flat vector-like styling, crisp sans-serif labels, direct arrows, and
generous margins. Do not add datasets, mechanisms, molecules, numbers, logos,
gradients, shadows, decorative chemistry, or watermarks.

## Final refinement

Remove the draft's dark conclusion banner and use the released space to enlarge
the four scientific modules. Preserve all remaining labels, values, symbols,
arrows, and colours unchanged.
