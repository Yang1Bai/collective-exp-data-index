# Main figure contract

- **Core conclusion:** aggregated regularities and in-domain correlations do not
  automatically transport, whereas a leakage-safe prior from a physically local
  neighbor can cross every originally specified point-rule gate within a
  controlled campaign; the gain
  decays with neighborhood distance and disappears under feature scrambling,
  but a paper-disjoint CALiSol test shows that the improvement does not automatically
  survive experimental-article heterogeneity.
- **Archetype:** quantitative grid with the KIT temperature-neighbor effect and
  sample-efficiency panels as the hero evidence.
- **Backend:** Python/matplotlib exclusively for drawing, previewing, export, and
  visual QA.
- **Target/output:** *Digital Discovery*, double-column <=183 mm width; editable
  SVG and PDF plus 600 dpi TIFF and 300 dpi PNG.
- **Panel a -- transport falsification:** Borg UTS--yield-strength calibration is
  strong in source data but fails unchanged on independent BIRDSHOT data.
- **Panel b -- selective local borrowing and external boundary:** frozen -20 to
  -30 degC KIT edge, three increasing temperature-distance controls, and a
  shuffled-source placebo, contrasted with the frozen paper-disjoint CALiSol
  primary, its numerically stronger 0 degC control, and its shuffled placebo.
  The common 5% gate, intervals, material KIT improvement, and failed CALiSol
  borrowing decision are visible.
- **Panel c -- sample efficiency:** composition-only target learning curve
  versus the n=30 augmented model; the augmented error is mapped to a point
  equivalent baseline label count, with the 22--50% post-outcome diagnostic
  interval shown explicitly.
- **Panel d -- artifact/condition gate:** ISODB has a strong pooled compensation
  pattern that is not explained by the simple Krug null, but family intercept
  shifts remain DOI-cluster significant.
- **Evidence hierarchy:** b establishes selective KIT effect and distance decay
  while directly displaying the CALiSol transport failure; c establishes local
  absolute utility; a supplies the coefficient-transport boundary; d
  demonstrates the anti-artifact workflow.
- **Statistics:** 100 target-sampling repeats, five formulation-group folds,
  5,000 hierarchical bootstrap replicates, 999 source-feature mappings, three
  learners, exact learning-curve anchor, plus the CALiSol 15-article outer
  split, article-hierarchical bootstrap, and within-article mapping null;
  DOI-cluster inference, Krug null, and family-intercept wild-cluster test.
- **Source data:** the release ships `figure_main_panel_a.csv` through
  `figure_main_panel_d.csv`, generated by the plotting script. Panel b is
  derived from `kit_temperature_edges.csv` and `calisol_external_edges.csv`;
  panel c is derived from `kit_temperature_learning_curve.csv`, the primary
  prediction records, and `kit_sample_equivalence_uncertainty.json`.
- **Image integrity:** all marks are generated directly from locked tabular
  results; no manual graphical adjustment.
- **Reviewer risks shown rather than hidden:** KIT is a within-campaign few-shot
  improvement, not independent-dataset rescue; CALiSol fails the paper-disjoint
  practical-borrowing and distance-ordering gates; independent BIRDSHOT lacks absolute
  rolling-time utility and Matbench is null; ISODB is a conditional regularity,
  not a negative result; KIT's label-saving point estimate is not an
  uncertainty-qualified 30% lower bound; the 0--3 global ordinal distance law
  remains unestablished.

## OOD decision figure contract

- **Core conclusion:** borrowing utility depends on the decision endpoint: the
  thermoelectric prior directionally improves fixed OBELiX OOD screening, but
  does not improve the frozen sequential-discovery campaign, while uniform
  random acquisition outperforms the tested UCB policies.
- **Archetype:** asymmetric quantitative grid with the official-test cumulative
  discovery curve as the hero panel.
- **Backend:** Python/matplotlib exclusively for drawing, previewing, export,
  and visual QA.
- **Target/output:** *Digital Discovery*, double-column <=183 mm width; editable
  SVG and PDF plus 600 dpi TIFF and 300 dpi PNG.
- **Panel a -- fixed OOD screening:** official-test and farthest-40% OBELiX
  candidate pools show the baseline and thermoelectric-prior fraction screened
  before the first true top-5% hit. Both are labelled directional; the hard-OOD
  subset is explicitly exploratory.
- **Panel b -- sequential effects:** experiments saved relative to target-only
  UCB are shown for the thermoelectric prior, shuffled prior, and random
  acquisition in the official and hard-OOD pools. The frozen five-experiment
  practical gate and 95% paired-bootstrap intervals remain visible.
- **Panel c -- discovery trajectory:** empirical probability of at least one
  true top-5% hit versus official-test acquisitions for target-only UCB,
  thermoelectric-prior UCB, and uniform random acquisition. Median acquisitions
  and budget censoring are reported in direct labels or annotations.
- **Evidence hierarchy:** c is decisive evidence that the tested model-guided
  policies do not accelerate discovery; b quantifies the failed rescue gates
  and random-control advantage; a preserves the distinct directional fixed-
  ranking result.
- **Statistics:** 60 repeated fixed-ranking target subsets; 100 paired
  ExtraTrees sequential seeds; 40 Random-Forest sensitivity seeds; paired
  percentile bootstrap; paired sign-flip tests; official DOI/composition-
  disjoint pool and outcome-free farthest-40% subset.
- **Source data:** panel-specific CSV files are generated from the frozen OOD
  screening and Balam sequential outputs. No values are transcribed manually.
- **Image integrity:** all marks are generated directly from locked tables; no
  manual graphical adjustment.
- **Reviewer risks shown rather than hidden:** fixed ranking is not sequential
  discovery; the hard-OOD screen was specified after the whole-pool direction
  and is exploratory; the hard-OOD sequential ExtraTrees effect fails the
  practical, consistency, and Random-Forest-sensitivity gates; random-search
  superiority does not identify whether mean ranking, uncertainty calibration,
  or iterative refitting caused UCB failure.

## Caltech external-policy decomposition figure contract

- **Core conclusion:** prespecified neighboring rankings retain selective
  external OOD signal after formula and DOI exclusions, while wrong-domain
  weights are suppressed. The same information does not survive target-refitted
  residual policies, showing that source signal and policy conversion are
  separate design problems.
- **Archetype:** three-panel quantitative validation grid with external static
  neighbor rankings and their adaptive-policy boundary as the joint result.
- **Backend:** Python/matplotlib exclusively for drawing, previewing, export,
  and visual QA.
- **Target/output:** *Digital Discovery*, double-column <=183 mm width;
  editable SVG and PDF plus 600 dpi TIFF and 300 dpi PNG.
- **Panel a -- admission versus source skill:** admission rate, mean source
  weight, and source OOF R² for OBELiX, ESTM, and the three wrong controls.
  The frozen 0.20 wrong-source admission ceiling is visible, as is the fact
  that the OCx wrong-domain control has the highest source OOF R².
- **Panel b -- primary adaptive null:** paired-bootstrap AUC20-gain intervals
  for the OBELiX, ESTM, and multisource residual policies relative to the
  state-matched target-only policy in both scopes. Every policy is explicitly
  labelled as failing the frozen decision family.
- **Panel c -- prespecified retrospective static ranking:** AUC20 and recall20 absolute counts
  for the two prespecified real neighbors, alongside shuffled, random,
  mechanical, and catalysis static rankings in both scopes. The panel title
  labels the evidence retrospective and outside the primary contrast family;
  the post-result portfolio is excluded.
- **Evidence hierarchy:** c establishes retrospective external feasibility of
  source-ranked OOD proposals; a establishes negative-transfer selectivity while
  exposing the source-quality confound; b shows that the tested adaptive
  conversion does not preserve that utility. The post-outcome portfolio remains
  method development rather than independent confirmation.
- **Statistics:** 100 paired campaigns per scope; 4,000 evaluated acquisition
  steps per single-source policy and scope; article-grouped cross-validation;
  paired percentile bootstrap intervals; one-sided paired sign-flip tests and
  Holm correction over eight contrasts per scope; exact formula and DOI
  exclusions.
- **Source data:** `figure_caltech_policy_panel_a.csv` through
  `figure_caltech_policy_panel_c.csv`, generated directly from the independently
  verified Caltech validation JSON, policy summary, and source-quality table.
- **Image integrity:** all marks are generated directly from verified compact
  outputs; no manual graphical adjustment.
- **Reviewer risks shown rather than hidden:** gate ordering is neither source
  skill nor acquisition utility; repeated campaign seeds are not independent-target uncertainty;
  static-source attribution was absent from the frozen primary contrast family;
  the post-result portfolio is excluded and cannot imply prospective discovery
  or new science.
