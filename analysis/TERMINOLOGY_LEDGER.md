# Manuscript terminology ledger

These forms are locked for the manuscript and supplementary information.

| Canonical term | First-use definition | Avoid or restrict |
|---|---|---|
| Collective Experimental Data Index | The experimental-first data foundation and claim-auditing workflow reported here | Do not use a larger discovery catalog as the manuscript's empirical denominator |
| analysed experimental-resource cohort | The 21 accessible resources whose numerical values enter at least one reported integration, borrowing, artifact, or boundary analysis | Use as the only manuscript resource denominator |
| component-order-invariant mixture relation | A source relation learned from a permutation-invariant, molar-weighted mixture representation with explicit temperature, concentration and state interactions | Canonical manuscript term for new-component formulation transfer; do not call the representation a microscopic mechanism |
| programme-balanced ordinal score | An equal-programme average of separately trained source predictions, computed without recipient labels and used only to order recipient candidates | The five recipient measurements train and evaluate recipient-only comparators; they do not enter the source score |
| programme-balanced fusion | Equal weight per experimental programme rather than per record | Distinct from reciprocal-rank fusion, which weights evidence by rank position; do not cite rank fusion as the algorithm used here |
| abstention | Per-edge, predeclared withholding of a transferred object when its required gate fails | Distinct from per-instance reject-option classification; do not imply that the current gate is a validated selector for untested edges |
| frozen source prediction | A prediction generated by a source model whose training data, features and hyperparameters are fixed before recipient outcome access | Do not refit it on recipient candidates or confuse it with recipient-only pretraining |
| shrinkage calibration adapter | A low-dimensional correction fitted from recipient anchors and shrunk toward zero so that the frozen source relation is preserved unless recipient evidence supports recalibration | Do not present it as a new source model or attribute the zero-shot transfer gain to the adapter |
| formulation-grouped OOD | An evaluation in which every temperature and concentration row sharing an exact component identity and ratio remains in the same split or resampling group | Row-wise random splitting is not formulation OOD |
| candidate donor resource | A resource whose fitted prediction, coefficient, ranking, or adjacent-condition signal is transferred to another task | Role denotes attempted information supply, not demonstrated benefit; a donor may also be a recipient |
| qualified donor resource | A candidate donor whose directed edge passes its stated source-quality, independence, control, utility, and endpoint gates | Qualification belongs to a specific edge and endpoint, not permanently to the database |
| recipient resource | A resource whose outcomes define the prediction, screening, or exploration target | A recipient may also be a donor |
| target-domain knowledge scarcity | A shortage of target observations and reliable mechanistic or calibration support in few-shot or OOD regions | Do not imply total absence of domain knowledge or treat it as one directly measured scalar |
| experimental-first | A design principle in which the unit of reuse is a reported measurement together with its material identity, experimental conditions, provenance, and reuse constraints, rather than a context-free composition--property pair | Does not mean that every source reports complete synthesis history or that the tested ML representations encode a microscopic mechanism |
| experimental context | The reported synthesis or formulation descriptors, measurement conditions, article or campaign provenance, identifiers, and quality flags retained with a measurement | Do not imply recovery of factors that the source did not report |
| audited candidate-edge inventory | The machine-readable collection of positive, null, practically equivalent, harmful, and unresolved source→target tests | Use for the underlying records, not as the paper's headline contribution |
| artifact-gated knowledge-borrowing map | The endpoint-resolved decision object constructed from the audited candidate edges and their evidence states | Headline methodological contribution; the current study instantiates this map over tested edges, but it is not a universal distance law or a predictor of untested edges |
| knowledge-borrowing map | Short form for the artifact-gated knowledge-borrowing map after first definition | “Map” includes rejected and unresolved edges; do not imply that every positive edge is independently replicated or prospectively validated |
| selective neighborhood-borrowing strategy | The six-step procedure that declares neighbors, qualifies leakage-safe signal, matches mechanism to endpoint, preserves independent source shortlists, portfolios complementary neighbors, and predeclares source-derived hypotheses | Headline strategy contribution; tested on two outcome-unseen retrospective targets, neither of which passes the complete gate; not prospective acceleration |
| neighbor-shortlist portfolio | Target-model-free round-robin or rank-consensus allocation across separately retained qualified source rankings | The Caltech portfolio is post-outcome method development; do not call it independently confirmed |
| CCA family-first policy | Credibility-gated source admission, complementary independent ranks, source abstention, and a first acquisition pass with one candidate per outcome-free identity/provenance or predeclared composition-family component | Method-development strategy for distinct-region OOD exploration; subsequently tested outcome-unseen on Starrydata and TRI and did not pass the complete policy gate |
| identity/provenance component | A connected component formed by shared canonical formula, DOI, or ICSD identifier | Removes obvious repeated evidence but is not automatically a mechanistic chemical family |
| discovery breadth | First recovery of distinct high-value components rather than repeated high-value entities within one component | Report together with entity recall so the breadth-versus-repeat trade-off is visible |
| source-derived scientific hypothesis | A composition, structure, condition, or mechanism proposal written from a source-ranked candidate before revealing its target outcome | Required bridge from retrospective ranking to “scientific inspiration”; not yet tested prospectively here |
| source-prediction feature | An out-of-sample prediction from a source-task model, injected as one feature into the target model | Use soft prior only as an interpretation, not as a different method |
| transferred object | The source-derived quantity that crosses the donor→recipient boundary: an absolute prediction, coefficient, ranking, or within-provenance response relation | Physical adjacency does not imply that every transferred object is portable |
| endpoint-routed knowledge borrowing | Selecting the transferred object according to the recipient decision: calibrated prediction for numerical estimation, a provenance-anchored response relation across scale shifts, or an independent ordinal score for candidate screening | Preferred description of the framework's central technical insight; do not imply that one transfer object serves all endpoints |
| independent ordinal rank transfer | Applying a donor score unchanged to order candidates in a provenance-independent recipient database | The claim concerns candidate order, not absolute-property calibration or sequential discovery acceleration |
| SolventSeg electrolyte programme | The independent LiPF6/EC/EMC recipient with 36 formulations measured at five temperatures | Spell out the chemistry and programme at first use; do not use the project name alone in broad-audience prose |
| FINALES electrolyte campaign | The independent, time-stamped LiPF6/EC/EMC autonomous experimental programme used for the frozen second-recipient rank-replication test | At first use define Fast INtention-Agnostic LEarning Server (FINALES); distinguish experimental ASAB results from molecular-dynamics results |
| frozen FINALES non-replication | The unchanged CALiSol ordinal score loses to the strongest three-anchor recipient-only baseline in the prespecified FINALES primary phase | Treat as a claim-bearing boundary and abstention result; do not retune the donor or call the SolventSeg edge universal |
| provenance-anchored contrast transfer | Learning a within-source-provenance response relation and using one or more outcome-independent target-provenance anchors to restore the absolute target scale | Required term for E6; it is few-shot and post-outcome method development, not zero-shot MatEx reproduction |
| target-article anchor | A recipient outcome revealed from the held-out article solely to calibrate its absolute scale; the anchor row is excluded from scoring | Do not describe as an unlabeled candidate or count it as a test observation |
| simulated label-poor slice | A target task evaluated retrospectively at the fixed label budget, usually n=30 per outer fold | Default prose term for KIT; do not equate with an entire data-poor field |
| relative RMSE reduction | `(RMSE_baseline - RMSE_augmented) / RMSE_baseline`; positive values favor borrowing | Do not report as accuracy gain |
| absolute utility | Positive held-out augmented-model R² under the designated split | Relative improvement alone is not rescue |
| target-equivalent sample count | Target-only label count interpolated from the frozen learning curve at the augmented model's RMSE | Do not call it observed experiments saved |
| target-label fraction saved | `(n_equivalent - n_target) / n_equivalent` | Report as a point estimate under the retrospective curve; the KIT post-outcome diagnostic is 21.84–49.91% |
| local task rescue | An operational table status for an edge passing every originally specified internal point-rule gate | Reserve for tables/glossary; prose should say “materially improves few-shot error and sample efficiency”; always note that the baseline R² is 0.739 and the saving interval crosses 30% |
| within-campaign | Source and target conditions arise from the same controlled experimental campaign | Required qualifier for KIT rescue |
| paper-disjoint | Complete source articles are held out from both target evaluation and source-model fitting | Required qualifier for CALiSol |
| directional replication below practical gate | Statistically positive external relative error reduction that fails at least one practical or utility gate | Reserved for BIRDSHOT |
| cross-article borrowing unresolved | Frozen CALiSol decision: repeated interval and multiple rescue gates fail | Do not shorten to external replication or significant transfer |
| post-outcome contrast repair | E6 status: the separately locked contrast-and-anchor method passes its gate after being motivated by the known CALiSol absolute-transfer null | Do not call preregistered, independent confirmation, universal transfer, or prospective discovery |
| coefficient transport | Applying a fitted source-domain relation unchanged to an external dataset | Distinct from source-prediction feature borrowing |
| artifact gate | A prespecified diagnostic that tests whether a pooled regularity can arise from coupled parameter estimation or inadequate conditioning | The Krug comparison is one inherited artifact gate, not proof of mechanism |
| conditional empirical regularity | A pooled association that survives the simple artifact null but still requires family or condition terms | Reserved for ISODB compensation |
| falsification-first borrowing audit | The evidence-labeling and gatekeeping procedure used to construct the knowledge-borrowing map | Use for the protocol component; the map is the decision artifact produced by the protocol |
| internal freeze | An author-controlled, time-stamped design record | Explicitly self-attested and not externally preregistered |
| prespecified given prior screening | A design fixed after inspection of a related endpoint but before the reported endpoint | Use for the OBELiX sequential campaign; do not call it independently confirmatory |
| source admission ordering | Admission rate and mean weight generated by the Caltech alignment gate | Do not call it source credibility: source OOF skill is not ordered the same way |
| practical gate | Frozen minimum 5% relative RMSE reduction | A p-value cannot substitute for this gate |
| OOD screening utility | Reduction in the fixed held-out candidate-pool fraction ranked before the first true top-5% hit | Distinct from average prediction error and sequential discovery |
| sequential discovery utility | Reduction in acquisitions required to hit the true top-5% set under a refit-after-each-acquisition policy | Do not infer from fixed ranking alone |
| directional OOD screening signal | Positive fixed-ranking effect with a confidence interval above zero that fails one or more frozen practical or consistency gates | Reserved for the OBELiX thermoelectric-prior screening result |
| OOD-discovery improvement | Sequential edge passing every frozen effect, uncertainty, consistency, model-sensitivity, and control gate | Not established in OBELiX |
| OOD-discovery rescue | OOD-discovery improvement that additionally crosses the frozen 10% shortlist boundary | Not established in OBELiX |
| uniform random acquisition | Prespecified outcome-blind random ordering of the fixed OOD candidate pool | A reference policy, not evidence that random search is generally optimal |
| source OOF R² | Out-of-fold R² of the source model under the relevant group split | Spell out out-of-fold at first use |
| Random Forest | Random Forest regressor with 300 trees for the designated KIT and CALiSol analyses | Keep capitalization consistent in prose; use `RandomForestRegressor` only for code |
| temperature notation | `−30 °C`, `ΔT=10 °C`; conductivity units `S cm⁻¹` or `mS cm⁻¹` as reported | Do not mix hyphens with minus signs or omit units |

## Evidence-status vocabulary

- `internally selected candidate; not externally replicated with positive utility`
- `directional replication below practical gate`
- `independent null boundary`
- `material within-campaign few-shot improvement`
- `paper-disjoint cross-article borrowing unresolved`
- `post-outcome paper-disjoint contrast repair; external replication required`
- `harmful`
- `practically equivalent`
- `strong but conditional empirical regularity`
- `directional OOD screening signal`
- `prespecified-after-screening sequential null`
- `outcome-unseen directional but unresolved`
- `outcome-unseen null or harmful boundary`
- `verified complete-boundary`

These labels are not interchangeable. In particular, statistical significance,
relative error improvement, absolute predictive utility, label saving, and
rescue are separate claims.
