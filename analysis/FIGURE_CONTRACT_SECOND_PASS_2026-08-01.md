# Main-figure contract after the second-pass audit

## Figure 3 — external relation transfer

- **Core conclusion:** A declared mixture relation crosses salt identity and
  database provenance with material predictive utility, but the recipient has
  partial representation support rather than extreme-distance novelty.
- **Archetype:** schematic-led quantitative composite.
- **Hero evidence:** zero-label measured-versus-predicted LiAsF6 conductivity.
- **Validation evidence:** formulation-grouped contrasts against state-only,
  chemistry permutation, salt exclusion, single-salt, and wrong-salt controls.
- **Panels:** a, directed data-to-relation-to-recipient contract; b, strict
  LiAsF6 prediction for 1,660 rows; c, comparator forest plot.
- **Reviewer risk:** the extension archive also contains four reference salts;
  every plotted and quoted row must use the corrected exact-salt subset.

## Figure 4 — endpoint routing under controlled perturbations

- **Core conclusion:** The same declared composition relation supports
  numerical prediction, ranking only, or rejection when assay, grid, and
  endpoint are fixed and a single chemical factor changes.
- **Archetype:** asymmetric mixed-modality figure.
- **Hero evidence:** aligned RMSE- and rank-gain forests with route labels.
- **Validation evidence:** donor and four complete derivative systems share the
  six-slot grid and measurement endpoint; five anchors and matched falsifiers
  are fixed.
- **Panels:** a, controlled-perturbation design; b, relative RMSE gain; c,
  Spearman gain and endpoint route.
- **Reviewer risk:** this is disclosed post-primary analysis; the figure must
  not imply prospective selection or independent validation.

## Figure 5 — ordinal transfer and abstention

- **Core conclusion:** A zero-label donor order materially exceeds recipient-
  only ordering across tested label budgets in one programme, while the fixed
  donor object fails to qualify in a second.
- **Archetype:** quantitative grid with one dominant model-comparison panel.
- **Hero evidence:** 15-row source/recipient/oracle comparison at five anchors.
- **Validation evidence:** label-budget sensitivity and frozen FINALES boundary.
- **Panels:** a, model comparison; b, 3/5/10-label sensitivity; c, primary versus
  frozen second-recipient donor advantage.
- **Reviewer risk:** SolventSeg and FINALES use different anchor policies; the
  caption and text must state this, and the post-outcome maximin sensitivity
  remains Supplementary evidence.
