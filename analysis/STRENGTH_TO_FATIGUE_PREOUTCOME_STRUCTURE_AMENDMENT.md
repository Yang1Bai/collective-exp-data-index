# Pre-outcome structure and primary-card amendment

The independently verified metadata-only release contains 17
provenance--chemistry connected components. One common composition links 34 of
the 62 eligible fatigue curves. Leaving that component out leaves only 28
training curves, so the originally named 40-curve primary budget cannot be
applied uniformly.

Before any numeric value from the `S-N`, `e-N`, or `dadn` sheets was read, the
following changes were made:

1. The primary target-label budget was changed from 40 to 20 whole curves. The
   40-curve arm remains a secondary analysis only where the outer fold contains
   at least 40 eligible training curves.
2. The formal primary knowledge card was narrowed to predicted UTS and the
   physics-aligned stress/UTS transformations. Predicted YS remains a declared
   secondary extension and cannot replace the primary policy.
3. UTS, hardness, and elongation source arms are deterministically size-matched
   to 530 rows, the smallest eligible donor count after excluding every
   recipient DOI. Full-source UTS is a secondary sensitivity only.

The endpoint, outer grouping, applicability gate, target learners, controls,
inference and success thresholds are unchanged. These corrections are based
only on recipient metadata and previously available donor labels.
