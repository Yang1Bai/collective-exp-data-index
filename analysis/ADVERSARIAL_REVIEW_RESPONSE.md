# Response to the 17 July 2026 adversarial referee audit

## Revised paper argument

In heterogeneous experimental materials data, we show that neighboring domains
can contribute useful and complementary information when sources are qualified
under leakage and wrong-source controls, matched to the decision endpoint, and
preserved as independent proposals. The resulting selective neighborhood-
borrowing strategy and artifact-gated map identify a material KIT predictive
gain, external Caltech OOD-ranking signal, and multi-neighbor complementarity,
while exposing policy conversions that destroy that signal.

We accept that the evidence does not support a universal topology of
transportable relations or prediction of untested edges. We do not accept that
a useful map must contain mostly positive edges. Here, “map” is explicitly an
endpoint-resolved decision object whose positive, null, harmful, and unresolved
regions are all scientifically informative. The present study instantiates that
map over the tested edges while keeping the distinction between component-level
feasibility and independently validated prospective acceleration explicit.

The post-outcome Caltech portfolio is not presented as confirmation. It is a
methods-development result showing that OBELiX and ESTM proposals are
complementary: their combined shortlist recovers 5/8 external top entities,
versus 2/8 and 3/8 individually. This supports feasibility of the strategy
component and fixed the integrated method for the subsequent outcome-unseen
programme; it does not revise the frozen adaptive null.

A further outcome-informed audit addresses the stronger scientific objective
of discovering distinct target regions. The eight external top entities occupy
four connected formula/DOI/ICSD components, while the three hard-OOD top
entities occupy one. CCA family-first consensus recovers all 4/4 distinct top
external components (AUC20 60 versus 47 for entity consensus) and both 2/2
hard-OOD components at acquisitions 1 and 2. Wrong-source AUC20 is 6/18 and
5,000 shuffled-rank pairs give conditional p=0.0020/0.0030. Entity recall falls
because repeats are deliberately deferred. This strengthens the exploration-
breadth mechanism but remains Caltech method development, not confirmation.

That independent programme is now complete. Starrydata gives a small positive
ionic-to-thermoelectric direction (+0.88% [0.02,1.77%]) but fails Holm
multiplicity, matched-source specificity, positive absolute R², exploration,
and all three hypothesis cards. The four-plate TRI OER target gives -0.079%
[-0.313,0.155%], negative absolute R² on every plate, and no confirmed policy or
card. The two-target mean is +0.304% [-0.617,1.225%], I²=76.7%. These results do
not confirm a general portfolio; they validate the map's abstention and rejection
logic under outcome-unseen testing.

## What would justify a stronger future claim

The next decisive experiment is not another retrospective target or Caltech
retuning. Freeze the complete CCA rule, component construction, novelty and
random safety channels, abstention criteria, breadth and entity-repeat
endpoints, and any chemistry-aware surrogate before a genuinely temporal data
block or prospective experiment exists. Compare each neighbor, entity
consensus, family-first portfolio, random, composition novelty, target-only, and
matched wrong/shuffled portfolios under formula/DOI exclusions. Write source-
derived composition or mechanism cards before target reveal and retain failed
synthesis or measurement attempts. A positive result with prospective units
would upgrade the strategy from retrospective decision-map validation to
cross-domain discovery acceleration.
