# Manuscript story and flow audit

## One-sentence argument

When recipient experiments are too sparse for reliable OOD decisions,
neighbouring programmes can supply useful missing knowledge, but only when the
shared relation, experimental state, transferred object, leakage boundary, and
decision endpoint are aligned and the edge is allowed to abstain.

## Reader-facing question sequence

1. **Why is the problem scientifically important?** Experimental discovery is
   most valuable outside measured support, where recipient evidence is weakest.
2. **Why are aggregation and conventional transfer insufficient?** Strong
   in-domain relations and donor predictions do not necessarily cross
   provenance or repair complete OOD units.
3. **What is the method's scientific intervention?** Transfer a qualified
   relation or ordinal score, not an undifferentiated database.
4. **Can that intervention improve complete OOD prediction?** Selectively in a
   controlled catalyst series, and strongly for an external unseen electrolyte
   salt.
5. **Can knowledge remain useful when calibration fails?** Yes, if it preserves
   candidate order and is evaluated as a screening score.
6. **Does the route generalize automatically?** No. A frozen second recipient
   rejects the unchanged score and defines abstention.

## Evidence ladder

| Stage | Evidence | Claim allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Failure baseline | 0/40 generic donor-feature edges pass | Database adjacency and generic feature injection are insufficient |
| Controlled perturbation | two numerical gains, one ranking-only edge, one harmful edge | The same declared relation can produce different endpoint-specific outcomes |
| External unseen component | 28.64% lower log-RMSE; raw \(R^2=0.629\); \(\rho=0.871\) | A qualified mixture relation can cross database and component identity |
| Sparse-recipient screening | \(\rho=0.910\) versus 0.537; \(\Delta\rho=0.374\) | Borrowed order can rescue candidate prioritization when five labels are insufficient |
| Frozen boundary | donor concordance 0.694 versus 0.783 | The method must abstain at the programme boundary |

## Paragraph and transition rules applied

- Every Results subsection now opens with the scientific test, not the dataset
  name.
- Each subsection ends by creating the need for the next test.
- Observation and interpretation are separated: numerical effects appear
  before mechanistic meaning or claim limits.
- Dataset abbreviations are expanded at first use and do not carry the section
  headings.
- The Discussion is organized by insight (relation), action (route), and
  evidence boundary (prospective test), rather than by figure order.
- The Conclusion states the operational rule once and does not repeat every
  secondary analysis.

## Remaining scientific boundary

The main unresolved requirement is not another retrospective example. It is an
outcome-sealed or prospective recipient in which donor selection, transferred
object, anchor budget, falsifiers, and decision endpoint are frozen before
recipient outcomes are accessed. The current manuscript can defend a strong
methods claim and strong retrospective OOD examples; it cannot yet claim
prospective laboratory discovery.
