# Figure contract: controlled OER relation transfer

Core conclusion: A donor composition–OER relation can repair complete-system
OOD prediction after five recipient anchors, but only for selected ligand or
metal perturbations; the same procedure must route other perturbations to
ranking-only use or rejection.

Figure archetype: asymmetric mixed-modality figure with a design schematic and
one dominant quantitative effect panel.

Target journal/output: *Digital Discovery* main-text, double-column.

Backend: Python.

Final size: 171 mm wide × approximately 127 mm high, matching the current RSC
double-column width.

Panel map:

- a: one 462-catalyst donor, four held-out 126-catalyst derivative systems, and
  the relation-plus-five-anchor transfer operation.
- b: zero-label full-grid ranking for spectral and composition donors against
  refitted shuffled-source nulls.
- c: five-label candidate-bootstrap RMSE and Spearman gains over the matched
  recipient-only model, with the final route for each derivative.
- d: unchanged donor ranking on the later 20-candidate sets, explicitly marked
  as selected-candidate corroboration rather than a discovery trial.

Evidence hierarchy:

- hero evidence: candidate-bootstrap five-label RMSE and Spearman gains.
- validation evidence: zero-label full-grid rank and later-candidate rank.
- controls/robustness: richer spectral donor, 500 refitted shuffled-source
  models, complete-system holdout, and a harmful derivative.

Statistics needed:

- Source-model out-of-fold performance is reported in the caption and Methods.
- Panel b shows Spearman rho; pale distributions are 500 refitted
  shuffled-source models and exact Holm-adjusted values are in source data.
- Panel c shows pooled effect and candidate-bootstrap 95% intervals over 200
  five-label anchor draws.
- Panel d shows Spearman rho and Holm-adjusted permutation p values from
  100,000 target-label permutations.

Source data needed: the frozen zero-label metrics, composition shuffled-source
results, five-label composition summary, and later-candidate temporal metrics.
A clean figure-specific CSV is generated by the plotting script.

Image-integrity notes: no experimental image is used; all graphical marks are
generated from released tabular results. SVG and PDF retain editable text.

Reviewer risk: the composition analysis was promoted after its planned control
was inspected, the four recipients come from one robotic programme, and the
later candidates were selected by the source study. The figure therefore
demonstrates a controlled mechanism and its boundary, not four independent
replications or prospective discovery acceleration.
