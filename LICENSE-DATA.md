appropriate credit to the "Collective Experimental Data Index."

## The datasets we link to

This project **does not host or relicense** any third-party dataset. Each
catalog entry records the underlying dataset's own `license` field as best we
can determine it (e.g. CC-BY-4.0, CC0, MIT, "Unknown", or a proprietary/
restricted term).

**Before reusing any linked dataset you must check and comply with that
dataset's own license and the terms of the site hosting it.** A link in this
catalog is a pointer, not a grant of rights. Where a dataset's license is
marked `Unknown`, assume all rights reserved until you confirm otherwise.

## Factual data notice

Individual factual fields (a name, a DOI, a URL, a year) are not themselves
copyrightable. The CC-BY-4.0 license above applies to the original authored
text and the curated compilation as a whole.
