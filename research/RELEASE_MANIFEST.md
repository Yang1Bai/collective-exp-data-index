# Research release manifest - 2026-07-31

## Included

- experimental-resource catalog (`CATALOG.md`, `catalog/catalog.csv`, JSON and
  map assets);
- latest manuscript, supplement, paper package, reference library, and writing
  audits;
- canonical four-figure bundle in SVG, PDF, PNG and TIFF, with figure scripts
  and semantic verification;
- frozen protocols, amendments, implementation records, analysis scripts, and
  Balam submission/fetch helpers;
- compact JSON/CSV result summaries, checksums, decision records, and
  independent verification outputs for positive, null, harmful, abstaining,
  and non-evaluable attempts;
- complete public attempt and analysed-resource ledgers;
- collaborator-facing database guide joining the 127-resource discovery
  catalog to 31 analysed or audited resources, including task-specific data;
- Edison Literature, Edison clean-sheet/hypothesis, Hypothesis Generation, and
  Legacy Kosmos reports, together with local audits and independent validation.

## Excluded by design

- local virtual environments, caches, scheduler logs, temporary downloads and
  scratch notebooks;
- local `Dataset/` and `data/` working copies of third-party sources;
- duplicated desktop Edison downloads whose hashes match the canonical audit
  package;
- large row-level prediction tables, bootstrap replicates, model checkpoints,
  embeddings and obsolete high-resolution exports that are regenerated by the
  versioned code;
- credentials, private TDM material, or access tokens.

## Interpretation rule

The repository is complete at the level needed for scientific review and
reproduction, not at the level of duplicating upstream data repositories. A
resource's presence in the 127-entry catalog does not mean it was analysed; a
resource's presence in the analysed ledger does not mean its transfer edge was
successful; and an AI-generated report does not override the locally verified
result.
